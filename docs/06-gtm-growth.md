# DESCO Nexus — Go-to-Market & Growth

## 1. GTM Strategy

**Beachhead (Y1):** 3 sectors (renewable energy, agri-processing, infrastructure/PPP) × 5 supply countries (DRC, Morocco, Kenya, Nigeria, Egypt) × 4 demand hubs (Dubai/GCC, London, Paris, Johannesburg). Density beats breadth.

**Motion 1 — Supply curation (mo 0–6):** Desco pipeline + government PPP mandates + 200 hand-picked projects, each AI-polished to investment grade. Quality bar: every listing must be a deal an analyst would forward.

**Motion 2 — Demand concierge (mo 3–9):** invite-only for 500 verified investors; weekly personal deal digest; white-glove intro service (humans + AI) to guarantee early "aha" moments.

**Motion 3 — PLG flywheel (mo 9+):** open seeker self-serve (AI materials free tier = acquisition hook), open investor waitlist, referral program.

**Motion 4 — Enterprise/Government (mo 6+):** direct sales to agencies, DFIs, banks; land with country portal or deal-flow feed, expand to seats + data.

## 2. Marketing Strategy
- **Content engine:** "State of African Deal Flow" quarterly report (data moat → PR → SEO); founder/investor success reels (native ad format for the platform itself).
- **SEO:** public teaser pages = programmatic SEO ("solar project investment Morocco" etc.), country/sector hub pages.
- **Events:** Nexus Investor Days (virtual, quarterly, per corridor); presence at AIF, AVCA, SuperReturn Africa, GITEX.
- **Partnerships:** DFI co-branded challenges, chamber-of-commerce onboarding drives, diaspora investment associations.
- **Brand:** Desco Global credibility + "Investing in Tomorrow"; tone confident/purpose-driven; FR+EN from day 1.

## 3. Sales Strategy
- Segments: Government (ACV $150k, 6–9mo cycle, exec sponsor + Desco relationships), Financial institutions (ACV $60k, 3–6mo), Fund teams (ACV $12k, PLG-assisted, 4–8wk).
- Team ramp: Y1 founder-led + 2 AEs; Y2 pods per corridor (AE+SE+CSM); Y3 partner channel (Big-4, consultancies resell government portals).
- Sales assets auto-generated by the product itself (live corridor data → pitch decks) — sell the product with the product.

## 4. Growth Loops (the machine)

**Loop A — Materials loop (supply):** founder needs teaser → free AI generator → output branded "Made with Nexus" → investors who receive it off-platform sign up to view the live listing → more investors.

**Loop B — Intro loop (demand):** investor gets matched → requests intro → seeker joins/upgrades to respond → seeker invites co-founders/advisors → more supply.

**Loop C — Track-record loop (trust):** deal closes → both parties confirm → public track record accrues → profiles become the industry CV → everyone must maintain one.

**Loop D — Data loop (intelligence):** activity → corridor insights reports → cited by press/DFIs → inbound authority → more activity.

**Loop E — Government loop:** one agency launches portal → neighboring countries' agencies demand parity (visible investor analytics) → regional coverage compounds.

## 5. Viral Mechanics & Referral System
- Double-sided referral: verified-investor invite ⇒ both get 1 mo Investor Pro + AI credits; founder invite ⇒ featured-listing boost.
- Watermarked artifacts: teasers, reels, and market charts exported off-platform carry live QR/link back.
- "Deal wrapped" annual recap (shareable stats: deals reviewed, sectors, response rate) — LinkedIn-native flex content.
- Waitlist status game: position improves with profile completeness + referrals (Superhuman playbook).
- Guardrails: no viral mechanics inside trust surfaces; invites rate-limited; quality-weighted rewards (activated referees only).

## 6. Investor Acquisition Strategy
1. Concierge era: 500 hand-recruited via Desco network, DFI intros, family-office associations (target: 60% monthly active).
2. Authority era: corridor reports + summit keynotes; LinkedIn thought-leadership program for top users (co-authored by AI, disclosed).
3. Scale era: lookalike outreach on closed-deal graph, partner channels (private banks, wealth platforms), localized campaigns per hub.
KPI ladder: invited → verified → mandate set → 3 sessions/wk → first intro → first close.

## 7. Founder/Seeker Acquisition Strategy
1. Free AI teaser/CIM generator as standalone hook (SEO + accelerator partnerships).
2. Accelerators, dev banks, chambers: bulk onboarding cohorts with co-branded readiness programs.
3. Government pipelines auto-create seeker accounts for mandate owners.
4. Success stories in local press (FR/EN) → aspirational pull.
KPI ladder: signup → readiness ≥70 → published → first investor response ≤14 days (activation metric).

## 8. Retention & Engagement
- Investors: daily digest push (3 best matches), streaks, watchlist alerts, portfolio news.
- Seekers: weekly investor-view analytics ("4 infra funds viewed your model"), AI update drafts, milestone nudges.
- Resurrection: "3 new projects match your dormant mandate" with real previews.
- Target: investor D30 ≥ 40%, seeker publish→90-day retention ≥ 55%.
